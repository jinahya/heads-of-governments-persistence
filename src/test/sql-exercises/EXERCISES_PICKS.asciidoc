==== HOG_HEAD_A_05: Predecessor and successor

For a person named exactly, each of their terms with who held the office immediately before and after it in that country; the incumbent has no successor.

.`head/03_advanced.sql`, HOG_HEAD_A_05
[%collapsible]
====
[source,sql]
----
SELECT *
FROM (SELECT h.*,
             LAG(name) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start)  AS predecessor,
             LEAD(name) OVER (PARTITION BY country ORDER BY term_start) AS successor
      FROM head AS h)

ORDER BY country ASC, term_start ASC
